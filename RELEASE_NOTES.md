## Doubt enters the world

**Most people do not think you did it.** Witnessing something strange no longer converts by default: each witness rolls their own verdict, bent by their grain. Lightning is weather to almost everyone — a skeptic shrugs off all but the impossible, the devout see hands in half of it — and only those who read the god into an event move in faith. A man hanging in the empty air remains very hard to explain away.

**Rumors carry stances, and spread.** A story heard is now a story owned: listeners keep a retellable memory of what they are told (belief finally travels beyond one hop), but whether they BUY the god in it is their own roll — a skeptic pockets the tale and none of the awe, and what they pass on carries their verdict, not the teller's.

**The storm remembers its victims.** Natural lightning now records who it struck, by name — witnesses remember "the sky took Feitreh", kinship and all, exactly as they would a smiting. Heaven and weather stay indistinguishable; that is the point.

**Thoughts stopped talking to the wind.** Idle musings now show as thoughts, always — the spoken voice belongs to actual conversations, to the pulpit, and to true cries: the drowning and the wolf-caught scream in their own words now too.
