# Manifestation audit

The simulation is deeper than the screen shows. This is the ledger of every
live system, how it reaches the player today, and the cheapest honest way to
make it *felt* in the world rather than read in a panel. The bar for every
proposal: **show it in bodies and places, not numbers.**

## The audit

| system | how deep it runs | how it shows today | the gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Faith & attribution** | per-person trust, conviction, per-witness divine verdicts, bold/timid forks | ledger %, codex stat, occasional speech | A devout and a skeptic are visually identical. The crowd doesn't *react* differently to a miracle. **This is the core fantasy and it's invisible.** |
| **Prayer** | kneeling, motes, an answer economy | gold motes, pink bubbles, a HUD count | The player has no inbox. Prayers are scenery, not asks you can answer — the ask→answer→gratitude→story loop is the god game and it isn't closed. |
| **Gossip & rumor** | typed memories, stances, multi-hop spread, fatigue | bubbles when watched, chronicle lines | The *spread* — the game's central mechanism — has no image. You never see a story move. |
| **Personality (10 traits, 6 mechanics)** | work pace, conviction, appetite, spirits, talk, endurance | inspector text, speech bearing | Nothing in how they carry themselves. A gloomy slothful glutton walks like a devout hardy worker. |
| **Kinship** | surnames, maiden lines, parentage, kin ties in memories | names, house tooltips, "your brother" in speech | No family tree tab (long promised). Families never *do* anything together visibly — no supper, no hearth hour. |
| **Colony pressure** | roofless/hunger/landless pressure, musters, marches | the departure notice, the walk | The pressure is invisible until the day they leave. You should *hear* the leaving coming for days. |
| **Legend (providence/dread)** | every act weighed into a reputation | nothing player-facing | The god has a reputation and no one will say it to your face. |
| **Morale/spirits** | drains, recovers, tavern evenings | inspector stat | Posture and pace never change. |
| **Health/harm** | wounds, healers, violent flags | inspector stat, healer vocation | No limp, no bandage, no sickbed visit. |
| **Witness reactions** | watch/recoil/approach by boldness & distance | brief flinches | Too subtle to read as meaning; not yet split by *belief*. |
| **Economy & seasons** | stores, trends, famine watch, season yields | piles that grow/shrink, fields, weather | Actually good — the model to copy. |
| **Interiors & sleep** (new) | beds, claims, doors | cutaway view, visible sleepers | Good now. Extend the pattern to work buildings. |

## The shortlist, ranked by payoff-per-effort

1. **The attribution moment** — when a divine act lands, the crowd *splits
   before your eyes*: those who rolled "the god did this" kneel or raise
   their arms toward the sky; the unconvinced flinch and go back to work.
   The Reaction system and the per-witness verdict both already exist —
   this is wiring, not building. One glance at a smite tells you what the
   village believes.
2. **The prayer inbox** — kneeling villagers get a soft light-beam marker;
   click one to read the ask (food, healing, a roof, a child); answer it by
   doing the thing; fulfilled prayers pay faith, gratitude speech, and a
   story that spreads. Closes the core loop.
3. **Shrine life** — believers drift to the shrine for morning devotions;
   congregation size IS the faith meter, in bodies. Skeptics walk past.
4. **The leaving murmurs** — when colony pressure climbs, "talk of leaving"
   enters the pressing-matter pool (the teller already speaks minds); the
   ledger gets a pressure gauge. You hear the colony coming, and you can
   intervene — feed them, house them — to keep them.
5. **Family suppers** — at dusk, a family gathers at its own hearth before
   sleep. Ten seconds of theatre a day that makes every house a household.
   (The family tree codex tab rides this work.)
6. **Trait-worn bodies** — gloomy shoulders, cheerful gait, slothful pauses,
   the glutton lingering at the pot. The inspector's adjectives, worn.
7. **The god's name in their mouths** — Legend shapes how the teller's
   prompts describe you: provider, burner, the silent one. Reputation you
   overhear rather than read.

Everything above uses machinery that already exists (reactions, verdicts,
teller topics, activities, hearths). None of it is a new simulation — it is
the existing simulation, finally allowed on stage.
